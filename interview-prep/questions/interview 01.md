# interview 01, your 60-second pitch
5 min. Type your answers under each item.

Almost every interview opens with some form of "tell me about your research". For comp bio roles, lead with the method and the problem, and let the fish be the example. A hiring manager at a human genomics company should hear something they could use.

A shape that works, one line each:
- The problem, in words a non-fish person gets.
- What you built or did (the method).
- The main result, with one number if you have it.
- Why it carries over to their kind of data.

(a) Write your pitch in four lines, one for each part above.

>

(b) Read it out loud once and time it. How many seconds did it take, and which word or line did you stumble on?
