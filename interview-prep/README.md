# Interview prep
Added [[11-16-2026]] (learner). Its own track, separate from the ML plan.

- Goal: decent at comp bio interviews (postdoc or industry) by February 2027.
- Budget: 5 min, every other day (even dates). About 15 min a week. Learner chose "a little now" over cramming in January.
- No backlog. A skipped session just drops.
- Format: one file per session. Blank questions in `questions/interview NN.md` (public). Learner's copy with answers in `answers/interview NN.md`, with the story bank and log in `answers/log.md` (both gitignored). Learner types answers under each lettered item, or pastes a voice memo transcript when the item says "out loud".
- Feedback in chat, no score: what landed, one fix, one follow-up question like an interviewer would ask. Push back on vague answers.

## The five kinds, rotated
- Pitch and framing: methods first. Problem, what I built, result, how it carries to their data. Fish are the case study, not the headline.
- Behavioral story: one story a session, in four lines (situation, what I did, result, what I learned). Build a bank of 6 by end of December.
  - Collaboration, conflict, a failed project or analysis, learning something fast, taking initiative, why industry (about the work, not about leaving academia).
- Stats and ML quick question: answer in 3-4 sentences, out loud when possible. Hypothesis testing, multiple testing, regression, overfitting and CV, evaluating a model, single-cell basics.
- Project deep dive: one design decision from own work. "Why this tool", "what would you do differently", "how did you know it worked".
- Coding read: a short pandas or numpy snippet. Predict the output, or write one line. Full LeetCode problems are outside the 5 min and stay optional.

## Rough phases
- Nov-Dec: pitch, story bank, quick stats questions, coding reads.
- Jan: more stats/ML and deep dives. Research talk outline in small pieces (one slide's message per session). The talk itself is bigger than this track.
- Feb: mock rounds in chat. Tutor asks, follows up, pushes back. Stories said out loud.
